ABOUT THE LAB

Understanding materials.
Developing devices.

ASMD Lab brings together semiconductor materials research and device modelling for energy, electronics and sensing applications.

Our research combines materials calculations, process and device simulation, and circuit-level analysis. We connect fundamental physical properties with the behaviour of solar cells, transistors, sensors and photodetectors.
